# ALP A* → SIDE / MINCO / Local-SFC / SCP Root-Cause Analysis

**Scope.** This is a source-level audit of the current tree and the already
collected `long_cylinder_forest.json` runs. No planner source, parameter,
scene, build, or simulation was changed or rerun for this report. Line
numbers below refer to the current working tree at the time of review.

## 1. Actual source call chain

The production SIDE path is implemented inside the `optimize_side` lambda in
`planner_manager.cpp`:

1. `computeInitState(...)` creates a SIDE MINCO initializer (around
   `planner_manager.cpp:1370–1450`). The requested offset is tried first and
   then reduced through the existing backoff loop (`trial_offset`,
   `side_generation_attempts`, around `1370–1515`).
2. The generated polynomial is checked with
   `finelyCheckAndSetConstraintPoints(...)` (`1518–1555`). Constraint-point
   state is restored after a probe; this is a static precheck, not an A*
   result.
3. When all cheap SIDE offsets fail but a dynamically useful reference was
   retained, the complete local SIDE horizon is sampled against
   `grid_map_->getInflateOccupancy(...)` (`1594–1607`). The first/last
   occupied samples define a local collision band.
4. `anchor_before = first_collision - 2` and `anchor_after = last_collision +
   2` (with one-sample fallbacks) are computed at `1626–1633`. The actual
   anchors are sampled from `astar_base_traj` at `1652–1653`; they are local
   rejoin points, not the mission goal.
5. `side_a_star_->AstarSearch(map_resolution, anchor_start, anchor_goal)` is
   called at `1713–1721`. On `ASTAR_RET::SUCCESS`, the raw path is obtained
   from `side_a_star_->getPath()`.
6. Raw points and the SIDE half-plane semantic are checked at `1730–1737`.
   The path is greedily simplified with map-resolution segment checks and a
   maximum three-raw-edge LOS shortcut (`1739–1768`). Turning points over
   20 degrees are reinserted (`1770–1817`), followed by at most two raw points
   nearest the collision band (`1819–1871`).
7. A sparse Local-SFC set is built from the simplified guide points before
   MINCO reconstruction (`1874–1957`).
8. The repaired guide is converted to chronological `guide_times` and
   `guide_points`, then to `repaired_inner_points` and
   `repaired_durations` (`1963–2033`). A fresh `poly_traj::MinJerkOpt` is
   initialized with `reset(...)` and generated with `generate(...)`
   (`2035–2044`).
9. The rebuilt polynomial is checked by the native static checker and by
   explicit conflict-window and complete SIDE-scope occupancy scans
   (`2049–2097`).
10. Only the narrow A*+Local-SFC path can carry a colliding initializer into
    Hard-Corridor SCP. The predicate is at `2503–2512`; the corresponding
    bypass branches are `2513–2534`, `2544–2577`, and `2604–2629`.
11. The candidate creates `CandidateSideScope`, installs Local-SFC planes
    (with retiming synchronization), and calls the existing
    `ploy_traj_opt_->optimizeTrajectory(...)` at `2655–2697`. The final
    checker and candidate result handling follow immediately afterward.

This is therefore one continuous chain; A* is not a separate planner output
that is directly executed.

## 2. A* output representation and safety semantics

`raw_path` is `std::vector<Eigen::Vector3d>` populated from
`side_a_star_->getPath()`. Each raw point is checked against the inflated grid
(`getInflateOccupancy`), and each retained segment is checked by
`segment_free(...)`, which samples at approximately map-resolution spacing.
The safety statement supported by these checks is:

```text
raw/simplified A* guide is free in the inflated-grid, sampled geometric model
```

It is **not** a guarantee about the continuous polynomial later generated by
MINCO. A* starts at `anchor_start` and searches to `anchor_goal`, both sampled
from the pre-repair SIDE trajectory. They are local rejoin anchors; neither is
the task's final target.

## 3. Simplification and whether it is the dominant loss

The current conversion is deliberately bounded rather than an unrestricted
polyline compression:

- greedy LOS simplification, with `max_shortcut_span = 3` raw edges
  (`1755–1764`);
- explicit retention of direction changes at or above 20 degrees
  (`1773–1817`);
- reinsertion of no more than two obstacle-near raw points
  (`1819–1851`).

Run logs for the obstacle-9 encounter contain many non-empty paths. For drone
0, representative records have PLUS raw counts of 8/12/13/19 and simplified
counts of 7–14, and MINUS raw counts of 14–17 and simplified counts of 10–13.
Most records also show `reinsertion_points=2`. Thus some geometric detail is
compressed, but the existing turning-point and obstacle-near safeguards are
active. The data does not support calling simplification alone the primary
failure.

## 4. Rejoin selection is a real topological limiter

The collision band is derived from 25 uniform samples over the **entire
`astar_base_traj` horizon**, not solely from the dynamic risk window
(`1594–1607`). The rejoin anchors are then selected two samples outside the
first/last occupied samples (`1626–1653`). This has two consequences:

1. The A* search is restricted to a local start→rejoin problem. It is not free
   to postpone rejoining until a later open corridor.
2. If the nominal/SIDE trajectory reaches a conservatively inflated obstacle
   near the intended rejoin, the selected goal can be occupied or topologically
   unhelpful even when a later rejoin is available.

The offline static check in `baseline_comparison_20260831/PATH_AND_VISIBILITY_DIAGNOSIS.md`
found exactly this pattern for obstacle 9: a nearer rejoin around `(31.5,
0.2)` is blocked by the inflated neighborhood of static cylinder 15, while
later points around `(32.0,0.2)` and `(33.0,0.3)` have a static path. This is
evidence for `BAD_REJOIN_SELECTION`, not `TRUE_LOCAL_NO_PATH`.

## 5. A* → MINCO conversion and the exact geometry-loss point

The repaired guide is assigned timestamps proportional to the selected local
interval (`1971–2009`):

```text
guide_times: original prefix → collision_start → A* interior guides
              → collision_end → original suffix
guide_points: corresponding positions
repaired_durations[i] = guide_times[i+1] - guide_times[i]
```

The points between `collision_start` and `collision_end` become MINCO inner
points (`2010–2033`). They are **not** equality constraints in the subsequent
optimization. The code itself documents the semantic boundary:

> The guide is a geometric initializer, not a polynomial constraint.

`MinJerkOpt::reset(...)` followed by `generate(...)` (`2035–2044`) produces a
new smooth minimum-jerk polynomial satisfying endpoint state conditions and
the selected durations. It is free to deviate between guide points. A smooth
polynomial can therefore cut across an obstacle-facing turn even when every
A* segment and every retained guide point is inflated-grid free:

```text
A* collision-free guide  !=  MINCO-rebuilt collision-free polynomial
```

The native checker then reports `STATIC_COLLISION`,
`STATIC_COLLISION_CONFLICT_WINDOW`, or `STATIC_COLLISION_SIDE_SCOPE`
(`2049–2097`). This is the primary representation-level geometry loss.

## 6. Local-SFC construction and protection reachability

Local-SFC construction occurs before MINCO rebuild (`1881–1942`):

- a plane normal is derived from adjacent simplified guide points;
- the normal is signed by PLUS/MINUS;
- `guide - normal * (2 * margin)` must be occupied, making the probe an
  obstacle-side orientation test (`1901–1905`);
- the plane anchor is `guide - normal * margin` (`1923–1929`);
- each plane receives a local active interval based on guide-point progress
  (`1907–1921`);
- guide/probe orientation is sanity-checked before insertion
  (`1932–1941`).

The resulting planes enter the optimizer through
`setCandidateLocalSfc(...)` (`planner_manager.cpp:2666–2677`), and the
optimizer emits Local-SFC rows using the same analytic MINCO position
mapping as corridor/static rows (`poly_traj_optimizer.cpp:1382–1431`). Native
static rows remain in the row set (`1433` onward); Local-SFC does not replace
them.

However, protection is intentionally partial. The frontend acceptance
predicate is:

```text
isCandidateHardCorridorScpEnabled()
&& astar_repair_applied
&& !local_sfc_planes.empty()
&& side_init_valid
&& side_dynamic_valid
```

(`planner_manager.cpp:2509–2512`). Any A* event that loses SIDE semantic,
fails SFC construction, fails dynamic usefulness, has no valid rejoin, or is
classified as an A* search failure is rejected before this predicate can
help. Thus a valid geometric A* path is not automatically an SFC-SCP
candidate.

## 7. Retiming and active interval semantics

The Local-SFC optimizer stores normalized ratios from the initial duration and
reconstructs active times whenever virtual durations change
(`poly_traj_optimizer.cpp:455–492`). Each row uses the corresponding
plane's normalized interval and includes the existing analytic time
derivative path (`1387–1429`). The frontend additionally scales active bounds
when SIDE uniform retiming is applied (`planner_manager.cpp:2668–2675`). This
keeps the SFC interval on the current time axis, but it does not turn the
planes into global or continuous-time constraints: they remain sampled hard
rows over their active intervals.

## 8. Encounter-level source trace

The existing logs provide two distinct failure layers for the final obstacle-9
encounter:

| Stage | Observed evidence | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| SIDE generation/backoff | PLUS and MINUS repeatedly end in `FAILED`; final low-clearance trigger reports `NO_STATIC_FEASIBLE_SIDE` | Frontend cannot produce a directly executable static-free SIDE reference |
| A* search | Many non-empty raw and simplified paths; typical records include reinsertion points | A* search itself often finds a geometric detour |
| A*→MINCO | Repeated `static_free=0`, `static_reason=STATIC_COLLISION` / `STATIC_REBUILD_INVALID` | Rebuilt polynomial does not preserve the guide geometry |
| Local-SFC/SCP candidates | Some records show `initial_minco_static_collision=1 collision_bypassed_to_scp=1` | The narrow bypass is real, but only for a subset of events |
| Backend SCP | Candidate 26 shows dynamics/model-agreement trouble followed by retry and QP exhaustion; candidate 27 reports very large actual jerk together with static violation and QP exhaustion | Secondary backend failures after frontend admission |
| Final selection | No record shows a safe candidate produced and then incorrectly rejected by selection | Not a demonstrated selection bug |

The baseline diagnosis records the strongest final-event statement as
`STATIC_PATH_EXISTS_BUT_SIDE_TOPOLOGY_FAILED`: an alternate later static
rejoin exists in the map, but the current local SIDE topology/rejoin choice
does not reliably expose it to an executable candidate.

## 9. Root-cause classification

### Primary

**COMBINATION: `BAD_REJOIN_SELECTION` + `MINCO_CORNER_CUTTING`.** The first
limits which topology A* is asked to solve; the second discards the topology
after A* has solved it by treating the path only as a MINCO initializer.

### Secondary

**`PARTIAL_SFC_PROTECTION` and backend SCP failures.** Local-SFC rows can
protect only candidates that survive the frontend admission predicate. The
ones that do enter SCP can still fail dynamics, trust-region/model agreement,
or QP convergence. Those backend failures are not evidence that the static
map has no path.

### Bug versus representation limitation

- `MINCO_CORNER_CUTTING` is primarily a representation/contract limitation:
  guide points are not hard geometric equalities or a corridor.
- `BAD_REJOIN_SELECTION` is an implementation policy limitation in the local
  repair window: the selected rejoin is inherited from the colliding SIDE
  trajectory rather than selected from a later feasible topology.
- The narrow SFC gate is an intentional control-flow restriction, but it
  creates a coverage gap for otherwise useful A* results.

## 11. Minimal fix direction (not implemented)

The smallest direction consistent with the current architecture is to make
the repaired local geometry a hard, locally scoped constraint (the existing
Local-SFC mechanism is the appropriate abstraction) and to avoid fixing the
rejoin goal to an occupied/too-early nominal point. Any implementation should
preserve the existing SIDE semantic, native static rows, dynamics rows,
free-time MINCO variables, trust policy, and final checker. This report does
not implement that direction and does not claim that a future change will
make every candidate feasible.
